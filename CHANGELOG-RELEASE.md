## 2.1.7

### WoW Forever

- **Binds / Grounded**: a new page under Utilities makes press/release key binds, the way the Grounded addon did. One key runs one macro when you press it and another when you let go, so a spell that is not ready never blocks the next one the way a single macro does (Raptor Strike on press, Mongoose Bite on release, say). Make as many as you like: the list shows each bind's key and the icons of the spells it casts, the macros save as you type, and a key that chat, the game menu, another bind or the weave key already uses is refused with the reason. By request.
- **Breath, fatigue and Feign Death on the cast bar**: a new switch under Nock HUD → Size & Elements → Cast bar (off by default) shows these timers on Nock's cast bar while you are not casting, in the game's own colours, and hides the game's bars. A cast takes over and the timer comes back after it. By request.
- **Trinkets on the cooldown grid**: Trinket 1 and Trinket 2 tiles show whatever you have equipped in each slot and its cooldown, in combat too. Swap a trinket and the tile follows. By request.
- **Traps share their cooldown**: as in the game, every trap now starts the cooldown of all of them, so one trap tile on the grid is enough. By request.
- **Cooldowns out of combat**: Feign Death's tile (and sometimes others) stayed empty out of combat and only showed once a fight started. Cooldowns are now read whenever the game updates them, and through the rank you actually cast.
- **Hunter's Mark in dungeons and raids**: where the game hides which mob is which, the mark icon now shows the game's own mark on your target, with its real time left, after you switch away and back.
- **No Trueshot Aura warning on a flight path.**

### Both clients

- The TBC and Forever addon files now carry the same version number again (2.1.6 shipped two of them as 2.1.5).
