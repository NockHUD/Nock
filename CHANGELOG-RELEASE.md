## 2.1.6

### Both clients

- **Spacing between warning squares**: a new Spacing slider under Alerts → Warnings → Appearance sets the gap between the squares when several warnings are up. The default is the gap you have today, so nothing changes unless you move it. By request.

### WoW Forever

- **Multi-Shot cast bar is back**: the live client reports Multi-Shot as having no cast time, so its bar never showed. The bar now runs from your press until the arrow leaves, and follows your haste from your own shots.
- **Hunter's Mark survives a target switch**: in combat, switching away from the mob you marked and back again showed it as unmarked. The mark now comes back with its real time left. In dungeons and raids the game hides which mob is which, so there it still shows as unmarked after a switch.
